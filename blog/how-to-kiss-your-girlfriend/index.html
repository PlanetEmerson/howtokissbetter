How to Kiss Your Girlfriend (So She Can't Wait for the Next One)

How to kiss your girlfriend so she can't wait for the next one: the everyday kisses that count, reading her mid-kiss, and passion without an agenda.

The Short Answer

To kiss your girlfriend, slow down and pay attention to her: look at her a beat longer, rest a hand along her jaw, start softer than feels natural and let her deepen it. To make it romantic, give the kiss a quiet moment and one true sentence; to drive her crazy, make her wait, build in stages and stop while she still wants more. Kisses that ask for nothing are what make her want the next one.

You kiss her every day. And still here you are, typing how to kiss your girlfriend into a search bar at an hour you'd rather not admit. Good. That itch usually means the kissing has drifted somewhere comfortable, and comfortable is where a kiss quietly stops meaning much.

I spent more than a decade choreographing kisses for the camera, then years teaching couples who'd been kissing on autopilot. The mouth was rarely the problem. The problem was everything around it: when the kiss happened, what it was asking for, and whether anybody was paying attention. This guide is about that part.

How do I kiss my girlfriend? Start with the basics

Look at her a beat longer than usual, rest a hand along her jaw, lean in slowly and stop an inch short so she can close the gap. Keep your lips relaxed and barely parted, softer than feels like enough, and trade between her top and bottom lip instead of pressing your whole mouth at once; how to move your lips when kissing breaks that down. Then halve your speed; on set, pace was my first note almost every time, and how to kiss slowly has the method. Give your hands a job (what to do with your hands while kissing has the map), save the tongue until the kiss asks for it (how to French kiss covers when), and let her decide how deep it goes.

That's the craft. The rest is about using it on the person who steals your hoodies.

How to kiss your girlfriend romantically

To kiss your girlfriend romantically, give the kiss a moment of its own: no phones, a long look, a hand on her face, one true sentence, then a slow kiss held a few seconds longer than usual. Romance isn't a special move. It's a kiss that is the point, not a stop on the way to something else.

The setting doesn't need candles. It needs thirty seconds where neither of you is holding anything: the kitchen while the pasta water heats, the car before either of you reaches for the door. Better yet, take her back to wherever you first kissed and do it again on purpose. A kiss with history attached carries more weight.

Then say something before you lean in. Her name, said low, like it's a complete sentence. Or the specific thing you noticed tonight, like the laugh she tried to swallow at dinner. Words from four inches away turn a kiss into a message.

Keep it closed-mouthed for a while; a kiss without tongue, done slowly, is often the more intimate one. When it ends, don't rush. Stay forehead to forehead for a breath, leave your hand where it was, and look at her like you're still a little stunned you get to do this.

Best ways to kiss your girlfriend in an ordinary week

Most of the kissing in a relationship happens in passing: at the door, half-asleep, in a hallway with your arms full. Those quick kisses teach both of you what kissing feels like now. Give each one a job.

The good morning kiss

This one says glad it's you. Nobody is exempt from morning breath, so keep it gentle: a closed-mouth kiss, a kiss on her shoulder or the back of her neck, then go brush your teeth and come back for the proper one. Kissing with morning breath has the full etiquette. Skipping it is the only wrong answer.

The goodbye kiss

This is the one most likely to shrink into a peck near her cheek while you hunt for your keys. Stop moving. Hold her face. Give it six full seconds. The Gottman Institute suggests a six-second kiss every day. Six seconds is long enough that you both have to show up for it, and it feels comically long the first time. That's the point.

The hello kiss

When one of you walks back in after work, find her before you find your phone. Arms all the way around her, a real kiss, a second of stillness. It says I've been looking forward to you, and it sets the temperature for the evening.

The kiss in public

This one says I'm proud to be with you. Keep it short and certain: three seconds on the lips, or a kiss on her temple while you wait in line. Calibrate to her comfort with PDA, not yours. How to kiss in public covers the rest.

The kiss after an argument

It says we're okay, even if the topic isn't finished. It is never a way to end the conversation early. Once things cool, ask, even if you normally wouldn't: Can I kiss you? Keep it soft and brief. If she isn't ready, that's information, not rejection.

The kiss when she's stressed or tired

This one wants nothing. A forehead kiss with your hand cradling the back of her head. A kiss on her temple from behind while she's buried in her laptop. Then leave her be, or go make the tea.

The goodnight kiss

Lights off, both of you half-gone, it's the easiest one to drop. Give it a few unhurried seconds anyway. It says today was better with you in it, and it's the last thing she feels before sleep.

How to kiss your girlfriend passionately and drive her crazy

To kiss your girlfriend passionately and leave her weak in the knees, make her wait for it, build the kiss in stages, wander away from her mouth and come back, and stop while she still wants more. Most of the heat happens before and between kisses.

Start hours early. One slow kiss in the morning, then keep the day's other kisses sweet instead of heated. Tell her quietly that you've been thinking about kissing her properly since breakfast, then do nothing about it yet. That's teasing with a kiss in its simplest form.

Build in stages. Closed mouth. A little more pressure. Lips parting. Then ease back to something lighter before you climb again, the way a tide pulls back before the next wave. How to kiss passionately maps the long version.

Take the scenic route. Leave her mouth and travel along her jaw toward the spot below her ear, slowly enough that she feels your breath before your lips arrive. How to kiss someone's neck has the rules for that territory. Then come back to her mouth like you couldn't stay away.

Stop at the peak. End it while she's still leaning in. Brush her lips once more, smile, let go. If she pulls you back, let her lead, and if it turns into a full makeout, pace that too.

Watch her the whole time. Fingers tightening in your shirt mean keep going; stillness means stop and check in. Driving someone crazy only works if she's enjoying every second.

Where to kiss your girlfriend besides her lips

Her lips get nearly all your kisses. In a relationship, the rest of her is where you can surprise her on an ordinary Tuesday.

  • The back of her neck, while she's at the sink or tying up her hair.
  • Her temple, from behind, arms around her waist, no words.
  • Her shoulder, through her sweater, while you're both on the couch.
  • Her palm or knuckles, at a red light.
  • The spot just below her ear, when you want her to forget her inbox.

How to tell what your girlfriend likes (read her, then ask)

You can learn most of what she likes without a word, if you watch.

  • She melts in: her weight shifts toward you, a hand slides up to your neck, her side of the kiss slows down. Keep doing exactly that.
  • She smiles into it: you've hit playful, not heated. Enjoy it instead of trying to make it serious.
  • She eases back an inch: too fast, too much or the wrong moment. Soften and see if she comes back.
  • A flat hand on your chest: pause and let her decide whether it goes on.
  • She turns her cheek or goes still: stop, smile, leave it there. No sulking.

Then ask, but pick your moment: not mid-kiss and not after one that fell flat. Right after a good one, or on a walk where nobody has to hold eye contact, is the easy window. Keep the questions easy: slower or a little more? More of the neck thing, or less? Best of all: show me how you like it. Then let her kiss you exactly the way she wants to be kissed, and pay attention.

Whatever she tells you, thank her and use it. Get defensive the first time and there won't be a second.

Asking for the kiss itself still works years in, too. Come here, I want to kiss you is a request and a compliment in one sentence. How to ask for a kiss has more ways to say it.

The grooming that changes how your kiss lands

You can't feel your own face from her side. She can.

Your beard or stubble. A few days of stubble is sandpaper on her chin and neck, and she'll remember the burn longer than the kiss. Shave a few hours ahead, or condition what you've got and kiss with lighter pressure and fewer drags across her skin. How to make your beard soft for kissing has the five-minute routine and the lengths that scratch worst.

Your breath. Brush, floss, drink water, and keep mints where you'll actually have them. Coffee and garlic don't care about your plans.

Your lips. Dry, flaking lips feel like paper against hers. Balm at night does most of the job, and how to make your lips soft for kissing covers the rest.

How to make your girlfriend want to kiss you more

If the only time you kiss her like you mean it is when you're hoping it leads somewhere, she'll learn the pattern. Then every good kiss starts to feel like a question she has to answer, and the easy way to avoid the question is to avoid the kiss.

The fix is what I call the No-Agenda Kiss. Kiss her the way you would if you wanted more: slow, hand in her hair, fully present. Then stop, smile, and go back to what you were doing. No follow-up. No eyebrows. The Gottman piece makes the same point about its six-second kiss: if it leads somewhere, great, but that isn't the goal. Do it a few times a week and kissing stops being a door she has to guard. That's when she starts reaching for you.

A few more things make you the person she wants to kiss:

  • Let her start it. If you always initiate, she never gets to. Pause an inch away, or simply wait, and let her close the distance.
  • Be close without asking for anything. Sit beside her, talk with your face near hers. How to make someone want to kiss you leans hard on proximity, and it works just as well in year three.

The first time you kiss your girlfriend

Just started seeing each other, or about to kiss for the first time as an official couple? Keep it simple. Pick a quiet moment when you're already close: the end of a date, the walk to her door, the couch after a movie. Look at her, let your eyes drop to her lips so she sees it coming, lean in most of the way and let her meet you. Short, light, closed-mouthed. Then pull back an inch and smile.

A calm first kiss beats an ambitious one; you have a whole relationship to build on it. How to kiss a girl covers the signals and the lean-in, the perfect goodnight kiss handles the doorstep version, and if one of you is much taller, kissing with a height difference sorts out the logistics.

When your girlfriend pulls back or the kisses went routine

One turned cheek isn't a verdict. She might be tired, stressed or simply not in the mood for that kiss right then. Let it go lightly and try again later with something smaller.

If it's a pattern, look at what surrounds the kissing before you look at the kissing. Is every kiss a lead-in to something else? Is something unresolved between you? Why couples stop kissing walks through the usual reasons and how to rebuild, one small kiss at a time.

If your complaint is that she doesn't kiss you passionately, ask yourself whether you want her style or yours. Some people like their kisses slower or softer, and some people just don't love kissing much. Ask her, away from any kissing moment and without blame, what kind of kissing she actually enjoys. The gap is often smaller than it felt.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I kiss my girlfriend romantically?

Pick a quiet moment with no phones, hold her eyes a beat longer than usual, put a hand along her jaw and say one true thing before you lean in. Kiss her slowly, keep it closed-mouthed for a while, and stay a few seconds longer than usual. When it ends, rest your forehead on hers instead of pulling away.

How do you kiss your girlfriend to make her weak in the knees?

Make her wait for it. Give her one slow kiss in the morning and keep the day's other kisses sweet, so the real one has been building for hours. When it comes, start light, build in stages, travel along her jaw toward her ear and back to her lips, then stop while she still wants more.

How do I make my girlfriend want to kiss me more?

Kiss her often without wanting anything after it, so kissing stops feeling like a request. Leave room for her to start kisses, take care of your breath, lips and stubble, and make the everyday kisses real instead of rushed. Wanting more kisses tends to follow from enjoying the ones she already gets.

How do I ask my girlfriend for a kiss?

Keep it simple and warm: tell her you want to kiss her, or just ask if you can. Inside a relationship, asking usually comes across as wanting her, not as nerves, and it's especially worth doing after an argument. If she says not right now, smile and let it go.

How do I start kissing my girlfriend?

Pick a moment when you're already close, like on the couch or saying goodbye at the door. Let your eyes drop to her lips so she sees it coming, then lean in slowly and stop just short so she can meet you. Keep it light, then read her face: a smile or a lean back in means keep going.

Why doesn't my girlfriend want to kiss me anymore?

Look past the kiss first. Stress, exhaustion, an unresolved argument, or a pattern where every kiss seemed to lead somewhere can all make kissing feel like work. Ask her gently, away from any kissing moment, and rebuild with short kisses that ask for nothing.
