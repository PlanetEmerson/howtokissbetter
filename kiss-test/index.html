How it's scored

Every answer moves one or two of eight dials by a fixed amount. The dials are weighted (reading your partner counts most, the room counts least) and added into a score from 20 to 100. Nobody scores under 20; taking a test about kissing already puts you ahead of the people who never wonder. Your archetype is the pattern that showed up most in your answers. Before you start

Questions people ask first.

Is the Kiss Test accurate?

It's honest, which is different. It scores what you told me you do against what I've spent years teaching (and doing badly at fifteen). It cannot see you kiss. It can tell you which of your habits would cost you points with a partner who knows the difference.

I've never kissed anyone. Can I take it?

Yes. Pick "Nobody yet" on the first tap and answer on instinct. The test grades habits, not experience, and the report tells you what to build first instead of what to fix.
