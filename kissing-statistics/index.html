The fine print

How I counted (journalists, this bit is for you)

This is a kissing site, not a laboratory, and I'd rather tell you that than have you find out. Every number above is real and counted the same way:

  • What it is. 468 completed Kiss Tests taken on this site between 18 September and 20 November 2026. Ten multiple-choice questions about kissing habits, scored from 20 to 100.
  • Who it is. Readers who found the test on their own, mostly while reading a kissing guide. They chose to take it and they graded their own habits. That makes this a portrait of people curious about their kissing, not of humanity.
  • What's left out. My own test runs and obvious bot traffic are removed. People who blocked analytics aren't counted, so the true total is a little higher.
  • Small numbers. Where a figure rests on a small group (17 Australians, 13 people in the bottom tier), I've said so. Treat those as gossip, not gospel.
  • Privacy. Everything here is an anonymous total. No names, no emails, no individual answers.
