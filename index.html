The moment, step by step

The best first kisses do not begin with a trick. They begin with a moment both people can feel coming.

  1. 01

    Create the opening

    Slow down. Get close enough for the possibility to feel real.

    Create the moment
  2. 02

    Read the signal

    Notice whether they stay close, meet your gaze, and make the space smaller too.

    Read the signs
  3. 03

    Make the move

    Pause close. Let the question be clear. Ask when words would make it safer or sweeter.

    Move with confidence
  4. 04

    Stay responsive

    Start soft. Match pressure and pace before you try to lead.

    Follow their response
