A sample lesson

The Mirror Technique, in brief

Start by matching before you lead. If your partner softens the pressure, soften. If they pause, pause. Once the rhythm feels shared, make one small change and notice the response. Good kissing depends less on showing what you know and more on noticing what happens next.

The book expands this into a practical method, then applies the same habit of attention to hands, breath, pace, first-kiss nerves, and long-term intimacy.
